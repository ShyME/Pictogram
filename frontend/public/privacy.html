Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Pictogram is a non-commercial portfolio project — a small Instagram-like app built to practise software design. This page explains what personal data it handles, why, and what your choices are. "We" and "the operator" below mean the individual who runs Pictogram; "the app" means the website at pictogram.imshy.me.

Data we collect

  • From Google sign-in. Pictogram uses Google to sign you in and requests only the openid and email scopes. From that we receive your email address, a verified-email flag, and the stable identifier Google assigns to your account. We do not receive your name, profile photo, contacts, or any other Google data.
  • Your profile. The username, display name, and bio you enter when you set up your account.
  • Content you create. The images and captions you post, and the likes and comments you add.
  • Direct messages. One-to-one chat messages are relayed live between the two participants and are never written to a database. The app keeps no message history — closing the conversation loses it.
  • Technical logs. Our servers keep short-lived request logs (IP address, timestamp, browser user-agent, the path requested) for security and debugging. These rotate automatically and are not used to build a profile of you.
  • Cookies. Pictogram sets only strictly necessary cookies: a session refresh cookie that keeps you signed in and a CSRF cookie that protects sign-in and sign-out. There are no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies, and no analytics scripts on any page.

How we use it

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To operate the app's features — your feed, profiles, posts, follows, likes, comments, and chat.
  • To keep the service secure and to diagnose faults (using the request logs above).

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with third parties for advertising, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who else is involved

  • Google — as the identity provider for sign-in. Your use of Google sign-in is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cloud — the app and its database run on a virtual machine hosted by Google Cloud.

Pictogram's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention

Your account and the content attached to it are kept until you ask us to delete them or until the operator shuts the project down. Request logs are kept only briefly and then discarded.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and delete individual posts and comments from within the app at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data, or to send you a copy of it, by emailing the address below. Because this is a hobby project run by one person, please allow a reasonable amount of time for us to respond.
  • You can revoke Pictogram's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Children

Pictogram is not directed to children. Do not use it if you are under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country if that is higher.

Security and a caveat

Traffic is served over HTTPS and access tokens are short-lived. That said, Pictogram is a personal learning project with no uptime or durability guarantees — please do not upload anything sensitive or anything you could not stand to lose.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of the page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
